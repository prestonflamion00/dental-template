FAQ

Questions dentists ask us

Do I own my website and domain?

Your domain is always yours. We host and maintain the site while you’re a client, and if you ever leave, you can take a copy of it with you.

Is there a long contract?

No. Essentials and Growth are month-to-month. Premium, which includes Google Ads, has a six-month minimum so the ads have time to work.

What about HIPAA?

We never handle patient records. Online booking runs through the scheduling system you already use, and website forms ask visitors not to share medical details.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Yes. We can move your content to a faster, modern site, or keep what works and focus on your Google profile and reviews.

Can you get us to #1 on Google?

No honest company can promise that. We improve what Google actually looks at: a fast site, a complete profile, and fresh reviews.
