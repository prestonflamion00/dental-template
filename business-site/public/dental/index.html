FAQ

Questions dentists ask us

Do I own my website and domain?

Your domain is always yours. We host, maintain and update your site for you as part of your plan.

Is there a long contract?

No. Essentials and Growth have a three-month minimum, then go month-to-month. Premium, which includes Google Ads, has a six-month minimum so the ads have time to work.

What about HIPAA?

We never handle patient records. The appointment request form emails your office directly and asks visitors not to share medical details. We never store what they send.

What about ChatGPT and AI search?

When patients ask an AI assistant for a dentist, it looks at your Google profile, your reviews and your website, and favors practices whose details match everywhere. We keep all three accurate, current and easy for AI to read. No one can guarantee a recommendation, but these are the signals AI relies on most.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Yes. We can move your content to a faster, modern site, or keep what works and focus on your Google profile and reviews.
